QuantiSheet 1.1.27
- 3D Model Quantities (new 3D Model tab, and the Takeoff menu): open an IFC building model - the open format most design programs export - and see its walls, slabs, doors, windows, framing, pipe, duct and the rest by type and storey, with the lengths, areas and volumes the model carries. Tick rows and Add to Estimate; adding again updates the quantities and keeps your prices. A model exported without quantities still gives counts.
- Ask the Project (AI tab, Review menu, Project Analysis tab): ask a question in plain words and get the best passages from the open specs or drawings and the job's RFIs, submittals, daily logs, meetings and issues, with where each one is. When the AI on this PC is running it writes a short answer from those passages only, citing them. Nothing leaves the PC.
- Draft Daily Log From Notes (Daily Log page in Project Center, AI tab, Review menu): type or paste the day's notes and add photos; manpower, work, deliveries, visitors, safety, delays and inspections are sorted into a draft daily log to check and save.
- Check Submittal Against Spec (Submittals page in Project Center, AI tab, Review menu): the spec section's listed manufacturers, the standards it names, its warranty and the submittal items it asks for, checked against the submittal's PDF - a first look before the architect's review.
- Project Risk Flags (Project Center Home, Project tab and menu, AI tab): late RFI answers, RFIs with a cost impact and no change event, submittals that can't arrive in time, activities past their baseline finish, overdue issues, old change events and hours lost to delays - each with the reason, worst first.
- Source code (GNU AGPL-3.0): https://github.com/Jondou6/quantisheet-source/tree/v1.1.27 - the full source of this build. Help > About shows the same link.
