@h1 About this guide | about
This guide lists what QuantiSheet can do and shows, step by step, how to do the main jobs. It covers QuantiSheet through version {V}. Features added in versions 1.1.17 to {V} are marked with the version that introduced them. To see which version you have, open **Help > About QuantiSheet**. If you have an earlier version, update first (see "Install and update").
Chapter 8 lists every command in every menu and every ribbon tab, so nothing is left out. Menu paths are written like **Takeoff > Check Takeoff**. Keyboard shortcuts are written like **Ctrl+K**. Inside the program, **Help > Features & How-To Guide (PDF)** opens this guide (1.1.29), and **F1** opens the Help Center, which has a lesson with hands-on steps for most of what is in this guide, and **Help > Video Tutorials** (1.1.18) opens QuantiSheet's video channel.
@h1 1. What QuantiSheet is | what | samepage
QuantiSheet is a Windows desktop program for construction takeoff and estimating. You open a plan set, read the specifications, set the scale, measure, price the quantities and check the bid before it goes out. After the award, it keeps RFIs, submittals, the punch list, daily logs, the schedule and job cost in one place.
It works on PDF drawings. Your markups and quantities are saved inside the PDF, so anyone who opens the file sees them.
@h2 Who it is for
The first time QuantiSheet opens, it asks "What do you mostly do?" and offers four roles: **Estimator**, **Project Manager**, **Service** and **Office**. Your choice sets which tabs and quick-bar buttons you see, so the program fits the work you do. You can skip the question and change your mind later.
@h2 System requirements
- Windows 10 or Windows 11, 64-bit (x64).
- 8 GB of RAM.
- Nothing else has to be installed. The drawing tools are built into the program.
- Optional extras, each a separate download: **Tesseract OCR** (to make scanned drawings searchable) and **Ollama** with the llama3.2 model (for the optional local AI helpers). Everything else works without them.
@h2 Install and update
1. Open QuantiSheet's page in the Microsoft Store: [[https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/9MZM8WD6CS7W]] and install it from there.
1. To check for a newer version, use **Help > Check for Updates Now**. In the Microsoft Store copy, updates come through the Store; if the Store does not answer, QuantiSheet offers to open its Store page, where you can update.
1. **Help > About QuantiSheet** shows your version and when the last check happened. **Help > Check for Updates Automatically** turns the automatic check on or off on copies that check for updates.
QuantiSheet is open source under the GNU AGPL-3.0 license. **Help > About** shows the link to the source code of your version.

@h1 2. Features at a glance | glance
@h2 Open, view and navigate
- Fast zoom and paging: fit width, fit page, actual size, marquee zoom, previous view and go-to-page.
- Big plan sets (hundreds of sheets): pages ahead of you are drawn in the background, text search works across the whole set, and sheets open without the window freezing.
- Auto Bookmark Sheets (bookmarks every sheet by number and title) and Link Sheet References (callouts such as 3/A-501 become clickable links).
- Ctrl+K command search finds commands, sheets, takeoff items, Tool Chest tools, RFIs and service records.
- One window by default, with **File > New Window** when you want a second one.
- Role-based tabs, a quick bar you can pin your own commands to, and a right-click page menu.
- Open Plan Set in QuantiSheet: right-click a plan room download (.zip) in Windows and it is unzipped into a job folder, sorted and opened (1.1.20).
- Watch Plan Folder: new sheets and addenda that land in a job's download folder are offered to you as the next step (1.1.20).
@h2 Scale
- Auto Scale reads the scale printed on every sheet at once; choose a preset; or calibrate to a dimension you know.
- Engineering scales (1" = 20', 1" = 20' HORIZ.) and the labels of a graphic scale are read too, and marked Check until you confirm them (1.1.21).
- Warnings for half-size prints and unusual sheet sizes; a scale read from a title block stays marked until you confirm it.
- Viewports give a detail at a different scale its own scale.
- A "Check Against Dimensions" option confirms a scale from the drawing's own dimension strings.
@h2 Manual takeoff
- Length, polylength, area, cutout, volume, count and wall area (length x height less openings), with totals by group in the Quantities panel.
- Rise/drop, convert between measurement types, show volume in cubic feet, yards or inches, copy several times, add more to a count.
- Trace Length, Trace Outline and snap to line ends and corners.
- Reshape outlines by dragging corners, select several markups at once, bulk edit, join and split runs, and draw arcs.
- Copy takeoff from one sheet to another; it is re-measured at the new sheet's scale.
- Typical sheets (count a sheet more than once), colour by item, and show only one item.
- Buy Quantities turns totals into sheets, boxes or rolls with waste; Wood Sheathing Helper gives the span rating, panel thickness and 4x8 sheets for an area (1.1.21).
- Tool Chest and tool sets you can share with your team as a file, including a metric set; live legends.
- Takeoff Standard checklist for your office's way of doing takeoff.
@h2 Automatic takeoff
- Auto Area traces a room with one click; Auto Takeoff Rooms measures every tagged room and checks each against its tag's square footage.
- Auto Takeoff Walls and Doors measures wall centerlines by thickness and counts doors by width; Walls by Role sorts walls into Exterior, Corridor, Demising, Shaft, Restroom and Interior.
- Count Symbol counts every copy of a symbol, including turned ones; Count Several Symbols, Count From a Picture and a Count Library you can reuse on every job; Show Uncounted Copies finds missed ones.
- Count by Label finds tags such as P-1 or WH-2, with wildcards for whole families, and lists every hit before anything is counted.
- Check Takeoff steps you through every automatic result so a person accepts or deletes it before it counts.
@h2 Trade and civil takeoff
- 3D Model Quantities (1.1.27): elements by kind, type and storey with their lengths, areas and volumes from an IFC building model, to the estimate.
- Roof Plan Takeoff: true sloped quantities and squares from the roof you drew. Aerial Photo Page: measure a site on a scaled public aerial photo, and export its markups to mapping programs. Import DXF drawings for takeoff.
- Trade Takeoff: pipe and conduit, ductwork, fixtures and devices, structural steel, drywall and paint, roofing, rebar and earthwork.
- Civil and utilities: sewer, storm and water pipe by size and depth band, manholes and structures, with trench assemblies.
- Plan and profile sheets: pipe length from the stations along the line (STA 0+00 to 15+41 = 1,541 LF), callouts drawn as linework read, NEW SSMH counted, and a structure shown in plan and profile counted once (1.1.21). Existing manholes marked for rehabilitation are counted as work (1.1.24).
- Work Notes Takeoff reads quantities that drawings state in words (for example, "INSTALL: 2 - 6-INCH PLUG VALVE"), plus fence, keyed notes and electrical one-line feeder tags. Compare with Earlier Set lists what a new issue changed, and notes are read about four times faster (1.1.17).
@h2 AI Takeoff (runs on your PC)

- Zones tell it where to work or what to leave out, and it learns your item names.
- It reads the work notes, traces rooms on scanned floor plans and makes scanned sheets searchable first. On civil sets drawn as linework, it reads the callouts for you, rotated sheets included (1.1.21).

- Optional local AI helpers (with Ollama installed): scope summary, find RFIs, translate markups and more. Nothing is sent to the internet.
- Voice commands for hands-free use.

@h1 3. Getting started: your first job | start
This walk-through takes about ten minutes and uses the practice drawings that come with QuantiSheet, so nothing you do matters. For a quick look at what each part of the window is, use **Help > Welcome Tour**.
1. **Open the practice drawings.** Choose **Help > Open Practice Drawings**. A small sample set opens in its own tab.
1. **Go to the floor plan.** Press **PgDn** or click the page in the Pages panel. Zoom with **Ctrl+mouse wheel**; **Ctrl+0** fits the page.
1. **Check the scale.** Look at the scale chip at the bottom right. If it says no scale, click **Set Scale**, click both ends of a dimension you can read, and type its length.
1. **Measure a room.** Press **Shift+A** (Area), click each corner, then press **Enter** to finish. The square feet show on the plan and in the Markups List.
1. **Measure a wall and count doors.** Press **Shift+L** (Length) and click both ends of a wall. Press **Shift+C** (Count) and click each of three doors.
1. **Cloud something.** Press **C**, drag a box around it, then type a note in the Properties panel's Subject or Comment.
1. **Edit it.** Press **V** (Select) and click your area. Change its colour or label in Properties. **Delete** removes it and **Ctrl+Z** brings it back.
1. **Search every sheet.** Press **Ctrl+F**, type DOOR and press Enter. Matches light up; press Enter again to go to the next.
1. **Read the Markups List** on the right side: every cloud and measurement you made, with totals. Click a row to jump to it.
1. **Save** with **Ctrl+S**. Everything is kept inside the PDF.
Good to know from the first minutes:
- Lost? Press **Ctrl+K** and type what you want to do.
- **Esc** always takes you back to the Select tool.
- AutoSave (bottom right) saves your open files every couple of minutes. After a crash, QuantiSheet offers to recover your work, or use **File > Recover Unsaved Files**.
@h2 Starting a real job
- **File > Open** (Ctrl+O) opens a PDF. **File > Open Project Folder** lists every PDF of a job in the Documents panel.
- **Project > New Set From Files** turns the PDFs of a job into one set with a sheet list; open it any time with **Project > Open Set**.
- **File > Open Bid Download (.zip) as a Job** unpacks a plan room's zip into Drawings, Specs, Addenda and Other, combines the drawings, opens them, reads sheet names and scales, and makes the folder a Project Center job. In Windows, right-click the zip and choose **Open Plan Set in QuantiSheet** to do the same (1.1.20).
- **Project > Watch Plan Folder** (1.1.20) points the job at the folder its plan sets and addenda download into, so new files are offered to you as they arrive.
- Open the specifications and click **Project Analysis** (Home tab or the Project Analysis tab) for the bid date, pre-bid meeting, bonds, completion times and the rest, then **Send to Bid Board** (1.1.25, 1.1.26).
- Then run **Takeoff > Auto Scale (Read Sheets)** to set the scale on every sheet at once.

@h1 4. How-to guides | howto
@h2 4.1 Move around a drawing | h41
1. **Ctrl+2** fits the width, **Ctrl+0** fits the page and **Ctrl+1** is actual size (Fit Width and Fit Page are also on the Home tab).
1. **Ctrl+mouse wheel** zooms where the pointer is. Drag a box with the right mouse button, or press **Z**, to zoom to a box.
1. Hold the wheel button and drag to pan.
1. **PgDn / PgUp** turn pages; **Ctrl+G** goes to a page number.
1. **Alt+Left** goes back to where you were, after a zoom or after following a sheet link.
1. **Pages > Link Sheet References** turns callouts like 3/A-501 into links.
1. **View > Enhance Thin Lines** keeps hairlines visible when zoomed out.
@h2 4.2 Set the scale | h42
Every measurement depends on the scale, so set it first.
1. On the Takeoff tab, choose **Auto Scale (Read Sheets)** to read the scale printed on each sheet and set them all.
1. Or pick a preset from the scale box on the Takeoff tab (for example 1/4" = 1'-0"). **Takeoff > Scale Presets** asks which pages: this one, every page, pages the same size, or a list such as 1-4, 7.
1. Or calibrate: drag along a dimension you know and type its length.
1. A written scale is only right on a full-size print. If a sheet says HALF SIZE, is a small sheet (11x17) or is a different size from most of the set, you are warned. Check a known dimension.
1. For a detail at a different scale, use **Viewport (Scale Region)** to draw a box with its own scale.
1. The status bar at the bottom right shows the scale of the page you are on. When QuantiSheet reads a scale from a title block, the status bar says "(read from title block - click to confirm)". Check a known dimension, click the scale and choose **Scale Looks Right (Confirm)**.
1. Civil sheets (1.1.21): Auto Scale also reads engineering scales such as 1" = 20' (and 1" = 20' HORIZ. under a profile) and the labels of a graphic scale (20' 0 20' 40'), including scales drawn as linework rather than typed. A scale found this way is marked **Check**: confirm it with a known dimension before measuring.
1. Small print (1.1.29): when a sheet has a graphic scale but its scale note is too small to read at first, Auto Scale looks again close up beside the graphic scale (for example SCALE: 1" = 5' or 1/2" = 1'-0"). The source column shows **close-up** and the sheet is marked **Check**. If the close-up reading doesn't agree with the graphic scale's labels, no scale is set.
Tip: always check one known dimension before trusting quantities.
@h2 4.3 Measure by hand | h43
1. Type a group name in the Properties panel's **Subject** box ("Interior Wall", "Carpet"). Every measurement you take goes into that group.
1. **Length** (Shift+L): click, click. **Polylength** (Shift+P): click each corner, double-click to finish.
1. **Area** (Shift+A): click the corners, double-click to close. **Cutout** (Shift+X) deducts an opening.
1. **Count** (Shift+C): click each item.
1. **Wall Area** (Shift+W): click along the wall, double-click, then type its height (9') and any openings (3x7 x2, 6x4). The quantity is length x height less the openings, in square feet.
1. Turn on snap with **Shift+F9**: the cursor snaps to line ends and corners.
1. **Trace Length** (Shift+T): click a drawn line and its whole run is measured.
1. The **Quantities** panel totals everything by group. A tool stays on after each measurement until you press **Esc**.
Useful right-click options on a measurement: **Set Typical Count**, **Wall Height and Openings**, **Rise / Drop** (conduit dropping 8' to a switch measures plan length + 8'), **Convert To** (an area's outline becomes its perimeter, a run becomes an area, volume or wall area), **Show Volume In**, **Copy Several Times**, and **Add More** on a count.
Also: **Takeoff > Radius** measures a circle from its centre. **Takeoff > Show Side Lengths** shows each side's length on screen (for the selection or all measurements on the page), and **Takeoff > Captions** shows the name and quantity, the quantity only or the name only on every page at once. **Takeoff > Convert Selected To** changes the measurement type of a selection.
@h2 4.4 Fix and organise your takeoff | h44
- **Reshape:** press V, click an area or a line, drag a corner to move it, drag the dot in the middle of a side to add a corner, **Ctrl+click** a corner to remove it. The quantity follows. **Ctrl+Z** undoes it.
- **Select several:** drag a box on empty space or Ctrl+click markups. The status bar shows their combined total. Right-click for **Move Selected to Item**, **Status** or **Delete**.
- **Bulk Edit Selected:** set wall height, rise/drop, status or item name on many at once; a blank box keeps what each one has.
- **Join Runs Into One** and **Split Run at This Corner** (right-click).
- **Arcs:** while drawing a run or an area, press **Tab**, click a point on the curve, then its end. Press Tab again for straight segments.
- **Copy to another sheet:** select the measurements, press Ctrl+C, go to the other sheet and press Ctrl+V (or right-click the page > **Paste Here**). Each is worked out again at that sheet's scale. Set the scale on the target sheet first; a measurement pasted onto a sheet with no scale is left out and the status bar says so.
- **Typical sheet: Takeoff > Typical Sheet (Count This Sheet N Times)**, for example 5 for floors 2 to 6. A "Typical sheet x5" badge shows and the multiplier is used in the totals.
- **Color Takeoff By: View > Color Takeoff By > Item** outlines each item in its own colour with a legend. Right-click a measurement > **Show Only This Item** fades everything else; Esc shows everything again.
- Where two cutouts overlap, the shared part is deducted once.
- Alt+1 to Alt+9 set the status of the selected markup.
- **Edit > Undo History** lists the steps you can go back to, and **Edit > Undo Steps** sets how many steps Undo keeps (0 keeps them all).
@h2 4.5 Automatic takeoff for rooms, walls, doors and symbols | h45
1. **Auto Area** (Shift+R): click inside a room and its floor area is traced along the walls.
1. **Takeoff > Auto Takeoff Rooms on This Sheet** (Ctrl+Shift+R) measures every tagged room and checks each against its tag's square footage.
1. **Takeoff > Auto Takeoff Walls & Doors** (Ctrl+Shift+W) measures wall centerlines by thickness and counts doors by width.
1. **Count Symbol**: drag a box around one symbol and every copy on the sheet (or the set) is counted, turned ones too.
1. **Takeoff > Count Several Symbols**: box one sample of each symbol, name them and choose **Find All**. You get one review list per symbol.
1. **Trace Outline** (Shift+O): click along an irregular slab or paving edge. Each click lands on the drawn corner or edge, and corners between clicks on the same line are added for you (Ctrl+click for a straight cut).
1. **Takeoff > Walls by Role** sorts walls into Exterior, Corridor, Demising, Shaft, Restroom and Interior groups from the room names on the sheet.
1. Then use **Check Takeoff** to look over what the automatic tools did.
@h2 4.6 Find missed counts and reuse symbols | h46
1. Count a symbol with **Count Symbol**. Its picture is kept in the PDF.
1. Right-click one of its counts > **Show Uncounted Copies of ...** to outline copies with no count mark in orange. **Count the Uncounted Copies** adds them in one step.
1. Right-click a count > **Add ... to the Count Library** to keep its symbol for every job.
1. On the next plan set, choose **Takeoff > Try the Count Library**, tick the symbols and pages and choose **Find**. What it finds comes in as counts in review, and copies already counted are left alone.
1. **Takeoff > Count From a Picture** counts a symbol from a picture of it (a legend snip, a cut sheet, a photo of a printed sheet). Results are checked in the review list before anything counts.
@h2 4.7 Count by label | h47
1. **Takeoff > Count by Label**. Type the tag and press **Find**.
1. Wildcards find a family: **#** is a number (P-# finds P-1 to P-12), **?** is one letter (D? finds D1 and DA, not D10), ***** is any letters (WH-* finds WH-2 and WH-2B). Without a wildcard, a tag matches whole words only.
1. Hits are grouped by tag. Hits in the title block, a schedule, a legend or notes are listed but not ticked. Untick anything wrong; double-click a hit to go to its page.
1. Choose **One item per tag** or **All as one item** with a name you type, then **Count**.
**Tags vs Schedule Check** (Review tab) compares tags on the plans with the schedule: type the tags to look for, such as D-#, and choose **Check**. **Make an RFI** turns the gaps into an RFI.
@h2 4.8 Trade Takeoff | h48
1. **Takeoff > Trade Takeoff** (Ctrl+Alt+T). Each tab is a trade.
1. **Pipe & Conduit**: **Find on This Sheet** lists each line style as a system, named from tags like 4" CW. Tick what is in scope, type over a name to rename it, then **Add to Takeoff**.
1. **Ductwork**: duct sizes from tags (24x12) with sheet metal and weight.
1. **Fixtures & Devices**: **Count Legend Symbols** counts every symbol in the legend on every sheet.
1. **Structural Steel**: members tagged W12X26, with weight and tons.
1. **Drywall & Paint**, **Roofing**, **Rebar** and **Earthwork** work from your takeoff (walls, roof areas, footings and slabs, contours).
1. **Civil & Utilities > Find** (tick "Every sheet with a scale" for the whole set): pipe is traced along its line and named from its label (8" PVC SDR-35), with lengths running manhole centre to centre. Manholes, cleanouts, inlets and valves are counted once each by tag, with depth from their RIM and INV callouts. Each pipe size is split into depth bands and gets a trench assembly (excavation, bedding, backfill, haul-off and shoring).
1. Every result is an ordinary measurement. Edit it, delete it, or press **Ctrl+Z** to undo the whole add.
@h2 4.9 Check Takeoff: review automatic work | h49
1. **Takeoff > Check Takeoff** (Ctrl+Alt+K). It zooms to each automatic measurement in turn.
1. **A** accepts it, **D** deletes it, **S** skips. **Accept Rest of Group** accepts the rest of the same kind.
1. It shows how many are checked. Accepted items show as Accepted in the Markups List and the Measurement Audit.
Only accepted items go into the takeoff and estimate.
@h2 4.10 AI Takeoff: a whole plan set in one go | h410
The takeoff runs on your PC. Nothing is sent to the internet.
1. Open the plan set and choose **AI tab > AI Takeoff** (Ctrl+Shift+A).
1. **Step 1, Read the Set**: every sheet's number, title, discipline and scale are read. Details, sections and schedules are skipped. Sheets with no usable scale are listed; set those with Auto Scale or Calibrate and read again.
1. Untick any sheet you do not want, then **Step 2, Run AI Takeoff**: rooms on floor plans; pipe by size and depth, manholes and structures on civil sheets; pipe runs and legend fixtures on MEP sheets.
1. Read the summary: totals and anything that needs a person. AI marks are dashed with an "AI" badge, and anything it was not sure of stays out of every total until you accept it.
1. Open the **Review Queue**, which lists everything still waiting, least sure first. Choose **Accept**, **Deny**, **Undo** or **Report Missed**. Or decide on the drawing: select a dashed markup and press **A** or **D**. **Ctrl+Z** takes the whole run off again.
1. Each sheet's scale gets a confidence score and a picture of the printed scale. A doubtful one is held until you confirm it.
1. Run it again after changes. What is already on the drawing (accepted, in review or measured by hand) is never added twice, and your earlier decisions are kept.
@h3 What it reads
- **Work notes.** The counts the notes state ("INSTALL: 2 - MANUAL TRANSFER SWITCH", keyed notes, one-line feeder tags) come out as quantities in the same run. A plain count is taken off. A length, a note with something to check, or a count another step of the takeoff already made waits in review, so nothing is counted twice without a look.
- **Scanned sets.** Scanned sheets with no text are made searchable in the same step that reads the set, so their sheet numbers, scales and room names are read and their rooms traced. One **Undo** takes the added text off again. If OCR (Tesseract) is not installed, AI Takeoff says which sheets cannot be read. Small-scale scans (1/8") are still the weakest case, so check those rooms.
- **The Estimate panel follows AI Takeoff.** Quantities it works out rather than draws (steel weight, wall area, finishes, door frames, work-note counts) show as takeoff lines with their CSI code. A work note's total is kept in one place, so a count cannot be added twice.
@h3 New in 1.1.21 and 1.1.24: civil plan and profile sets
- **Callouts drawn as linework.** On civil sets whose callouts and scales are drawn as lines rather than typed text, the sheets are made searchable the way they are shown, rotated sheets included, and a quarter turn round as well, so callouts that run up a profile are read too.
- **Pipe length from the stations.** When the traced line does not agree with the stations along it, the length comes from the stations (STA 0+00 to 15+41 = 1,541 LF), carried across the sheets of one line. Labels with a restrained or mechanical joint (6" RJ PVC) and lengths with commas (1,541 LF) are read, and a size missing from a scanned label is taken from the line type tags (N6FM) when they all agree.
- **Structures.** NEW SSMH is counted as a new manhole, and a callout shown in the plan and again up the profile at the same station is counted once.
- **Manhole rehabilitation (1.1.24).** An existing manhole marked for lining or coating is counted as work, Manhole rehabilitation (existing manhole), instead of being left as an existing structure, including when it is only on a site or demolition plan.

@h2 4.11 Work Notes Takeoff: what the sheets say | h411
Rehab and upfit drawings often state quantities in words rather than linework. Work Notes Takeoff reads them into a list.
1. Open the drawing set, then choose **Civil > Work Notes Takeoff** and **Read the Drawings**. **Totals** adds the same item up across the sheets; **Every row** lists each one with its sheet. Double-click a row to go to that sheet.
1. The **From** column says where a row came from: a sheet note, the summary table, a numbered or keyed note, the fence line measured as drawn, an electrical one-line diagram, or worked out (the removal half of a "remove and replace").
1. Choose **Specification** to work out fence posts and barbed wire strands from the specification's own rules (post spacing, number of strands). A rule the specification does not give is not applied.
1. Yellow rows need a look, and **Check** says why: no number in the note (counted as 1), marked approximate, the same note twice on a sheet, a count taken from symbols, or a measured length that differs from the note.
1. **Export** writes the totals and every row to a spreadsheet.
1. **Add to Estimate** puts the totals in the Estimate panel as lines to price, each with a note saying which sheets it came from and what to check. The lines are linked to the same totals AI Takeoff uses, so a note's count is never added twice, and adding again updates the quantity and keeps the price you typed.
1. **Compare with Earlier Set** (1.1.17): pick the earlier issue of the drawings. A **Changes** tab lists every total the new set changed (new, gone or a different count) and the sheets where it changed, for example +12 LF of fence on C-7, or a gate gone.
What it reads:
- **One-line diagrams:** feeder tags such as 4 #2, #8G., 1 1/4"C. give the conduit, wire and ground sizes of each feeder, listed once per feeder. The length shows as "measure" because the route is not drawn.
- **TYP keyed notes:** a keyed note whose symbol is marked TYP means one symbol stands for every one like it. The count follows the note's leader to the symbol it points at and counts every symbol like it on the sheet with the same label beside it. The row says how it was counted.
- **Fences:** a gate whose width the notes give (16-FOOT WIDE GATE) comes out of the measured fence line as a Gate Openings row, and posts and barbed wire are worked out on the line without it. A note that replaces only the barbed wire (SITE FENCING BARBED WIRE) gets the strands alone, as Strand Barbed Wire Replaced, with no fence posts.
- **Nothing to count (1.1.21):** a note such as an INSTALL with only a station after it, or pipe named with no length, no longer adds a 1 EA line.
- **Speed (1.1.17):** notes are read about four times faster (on a 32-sheet set, 31 seconds down to 8), here and in AI Takeoff.
It reads what is written. Conduit and wire routes that are not drawn, and sizes that are on a standard detail outside the set, are not in the list. A length shown as "measure" has its make-up from the notes but no length, so measure the run yourself.

- **View > Snap Modes** picks what the cursor snaps to (ends, corners, perpendicular and more) and **Snap to Content** turns snapping to the drawing on or off. **View > Color Takeoff By > Priced / Not Priced** shows at a glance what still needs a price. **View > Double-Click Edits PDF Text** turns double-click text editing on or off. **View > Ribbon Tabs > Classic Tabs** brings back all the tabs as they were before roles (at the next start).
- **View > Field Mode** for a tablet on site: bigger touch targets, larger text, the ribbon hidden and the dark theme. **Density** (compact, comfortable or touch) and **Theme** (light, dark or follow Windows) also work on their own (both themes use QuantiSheet red for buttons and tiles and for done, won, ready and OK, 1.1.30), and **View > Layout** offers Modern or Full Ribbon + Tool Rail. **View > Show Ribbon** and **Bottom Panels** (F9) show or hide them.
@h2 4.38 Track deliveries | h430
**Tools > Package Tracker**: type or paste tracking numbers, or paste a whole shipping email and the numbers are picked out. Add what it is, who it is from, the job and the expected day. Select a package and click **Track** to open the carrier's own tracking page; a package past its expected day shows LATE in red.

@h2 4.39 Generate a starter set | hgen
**File > Create > Generate Set** turns a short brief into a ten-sheet starter set: cover, notes, site plan, floor plan, roof plan, elevations, section, schedules and a quantity summary for the estimate (eleven sheets for a two-story house).
1. Type a brief such as "3 bed 2 bath ranch, about 1,600 sf, new". The fields below the brief win over it: building type, new or existing, gross area (400 to 8,000 SF), occupancy, name, address and firm.
1. A sketch (PDF or image) is optional; its outline proportions and any typed room names are read.
1. Add a 1-, 2- or 3-car garage, a covered porch or a rear deck, two stories, and the lot and setbacks for the site plan. Anything missing is marked ASSUMED.
1. Pick where to save. The set opens when it is done. Its quantities are saved as a .quantities.csv beside the PDF and travel inside it, so the Quantities panel lists them ready for a unit cost and waste.
Every sheet says FOR REVIEW - NOT FOR SEALING. It is a starting point for a licensed design professional, not a permit set.

@h1 6. Tips, FAQ and troubleshooting | faq
**Do I need an internet connection or an AI model to use QuantiSheet?** No. Everything works without the optional local AI helpers. AI Takeoff and Project Analysis run on your PC, and nothing is sent to the internet. The optional text helpers, such as Summarize with Local AI, use Ollama running on your PC.
**Why is an AI result not in my totals?** AI results are dashed with an "AI" badge until you accept them. Select one and press A to accept it or D to deny it, or work through the Review Queue. Only accepted items go into the takeoff and estimate. The bid check warns while AI items are still waiting for review.
**My quantities look wrong.** Check the scale first. Click the scale in the status bar, measure a dimension you know, and confirm or re-set it. Sheets marked HALF SIZE, 11x17 sheets and sheets of a different size from the rest of the set can make a written scale wrong.
**A scale says Check (1.1.21).** Engineering scales and graphic scales read from civil sheets are marked Check because they are read from the drawing rather than typed. Measure a dimension you know, then confirm the scale.
**A scanned plan will not search, or AI Takeoff cannot read a scanned sheet.** Run **Document > Make Searchable (OCR)**. AI Takeoff does this for scanned sheets with no text as part of reading the set (one Undo takes the text off again). OCR needs Tesseract installed on the PC; if it is missing, AI Takeoff says which sheets it cannot read. Scans with small scales (such as 1/8" = 1'-0") and rooms beside casework are still the weakest case, so check those rooms.
**Project Analysis says not found.** It only answers what the specifications say, and it never guesses. Double-click any answer to check its page. A budget is often not in the specifications at all. If the whole list is not found, the PDF is probably scanned: run **Make Searchable (OCR)** first, then **Run Again**. After adding an addendum to the PDF, Run Again so the newest addendum wins.
**Send to Bid Board or Fill From Specs left a field empty.** Only answers found in the specifications are filled, and Fill From Specs never replaces what you typed. Type the rest yourself before you save.
**A form's total does not change.** A form works out only the totals its maker set up, and only when you fill a field. A total with no calculation behind it stays as typed, so type it yourself.
**Can I use the calculators for design?** No. The structural calculators are marked preliminary: they are for estimating. The engineer of record's drawings and calculations come first.
**I pasted takeoff on another sheet and it is missing.** A measurement pasted onto a sheet with no scale is left out, and the status bar says so. Set the scale on that sheet first, then paste again.
**I made a mistake.** Ctrl+Z undoes it; the Undo command says what it takes back. A whole AI Takeoff run can be undone in one step. A redaction can be undone until you save.
**QuantiSheet closed unexpectedly.** The next start offers to recover your work, or use **File > Recover Unsaved Files**. **File > Version History** lists earlier saved versions.
**The window stopped responding.** If the window stops responding for 10 seconds, QuantiSheet writes what it was doing to crash.log in your .quantisheet folder. You can attach it when you contact support. After a freeze, the next start asks if you want to tell support about it (**Don't Ask About Freezes** turns that off).
**A saved estimate layout or tag could not be read.** Saving a layout or a tag then offers **Keep a Copy** and **Start Fresh**. The unreadable data stays in the PDF under another name, unchanged, and the job starts with no layouts or tags of its own.
**Check for Updates does not work in the Microsoft Store copy.** If the Store does not answer, QuantiSheet offers to open its page in the Store, where Update works. A copy installed from a package file instead of the Store says the Store cannot update it.
**A big plan set is slow.** Scales and sheet names are read in the background so a large set opens without freezing, and setting a scale, rotating pages and undoing them are fast on large files. To email a big set, use **File > Save As > Reduced Size PDF**.
@h2 Tips
- Use Ctrl+K whenever you cannot find a button.
- Run **Project Analysis** on the specifications as soon as they arrive, then **Send to Bid Board**.
- Give every measurement a group name in the Subject box before you start.
- Run Check Takeoff after every automatic tool.
- Save a styled markup to the Tool Chest (right-click > Add to Tool Chest) to reuse it with one click.
- Use Show Unpriced Markups and the Bid Submittal Checklist before you lock a bid.
- For big sets: Auto Bookmark Sheets and Link Sheet References give you a sheet index and clickable callouts.

@h1 7. Help and support | help
**In the program:** **F1** opens the Help Center with step-by-step lessons and a **Show Me** button that opens the practice drawings and starts the command for you. **Help > Welcome Tour** gives a short tour, and **Help > Video Tutorials** opens QuantiSheet's video channel (1.1.18). **Help > Features & How-To Guide (PDF)** (1.1.29) opens this guide in a tab; it is also a **Guide** button on the Help tab and at the top of the Help Center. It opens from a read-only copy, so it can't be changed by accident, and the install keeps it in its help folder.
**Report a problem:** **Help > Report an Issue**. Say what you were doing, what you expected and what happened, check the screenshot (keep it, retake it or leave it out) and click **Open the Email**. Your email program opens addressed to support with everything attached. Nothing is sent until you click Send. Facts about the open drawing (page count, sheet sizes, how many markups, file size) go with it, never the drawing's content, and you choose whether to attach the drawing itself. Logs that go along have names, emails and network paths taken out.
**Help menu:** **Help Center & Tutorials** (F1), **Features & How-To Guide (PDF)**, **Practice Job (10 Minutes)** (a whole first job on a made-up plan: open, scale, measure, price, export), **Keyboard Shortcuts**, **Customize Shortcuts**, **Recent Problems (Log)** (the last warnings and errors, with Copy), **Support Bundle for IT** (a zip for your help desk with versions, settings that IT set, what is working and the logs; no keys, passwords or email addresses), **Add the QuantiSheet Printer**, **Enter License Key**, **Terms of Service**, **Privacy** and **Third-Party Licenses** (the open source parts QuantiSheet uses and their licenses).
**Version and source code:** **Help > About QuantiSheet** shows your version and the link to the source code (GNU AGPL-3.0).
