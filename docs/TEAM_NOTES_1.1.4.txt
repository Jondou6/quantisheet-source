QuantiSheet 1.1.4
- Exclusions side by side: who excludes what. A new Exclusions tab in the Leveling window, and a button on the Leveling Board and on Scope Sheets (Compare Exclusions). One row per exclusion, one column per bidder; each cell says excluded, included or not stated, in the bidder's own words. An exclusion only one bidder has is marked ONLY ONE. Similar wordings share a row: right-click to split one off or merge two rows. It exports with the leveling report. One click prices an exclusion: on a Scope Sheet it puts the $ on that bidder's exclusion row (still Pending until you accept it); in the Leveling window it adds an adjustment.
- Qualification at a glance: each bidder shows insurance, license, bonding against this bid (single-job limit) and prequalification, from the Company Directory, on the Leveling window, Leveling Board, Scope Sheets and the Bid-Day sheet. Awarding to a bidder with expired insurance or license, or a bid over the bonding or approved limit, asks for a reason first; the reason is kept with the award. The Company Directory has a new License expires date.
- Show where a number came from: every value read from a sub's proposal keeps its page and place. Right-click a price in the Leveling matrix or on the Leveling Board, press Show Source on a Scope Sheet row, or double-click a cell in the Exclusions table, and the quote opens with the words boxed. Works on scanned quotes when OCR is installed.
- Read with AI, for a proposal the program couldn't read in full (lines not read, or lines that don't add up): Read a Proposal with AI in the Leveling window, Read a Quote with AI on the Leveling Board, Import with AI on a Scope Sheet. The AI on this PC reads it; nothing leaves the computer, and it runs only when you ask, with a Stop button and a 5-minute limit. Only values that are in the quote word for word are offered; anything else the model says is thrown out and counted. Each offer is marked AI, check and is added only when you tick it. Lines that don't add up to the total are flagged, never changed to fit. It is still a draft to check against the quote.
