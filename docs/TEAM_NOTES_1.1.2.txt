QuantiSheet 1.1.2
- New look: green ribbon tiles, a PDF Management tab with every PDF tool in one place (Edit PDF first), an AI tab, and the Takeoff tab always on the ribbon. The window's title bar follows the dark theme.
- Service: invoices now carry sales tax (pick the rate; "Make default"), a member discount and a credit card fee, each on its own line and in the export to Back Office, Sage and hh2. New Service Settings window (Service > Service Settings) for the card fee %, default tax, invoice terms, member discount and agreement visits. Agreements keep the discount they were sold with.
- Service, also new: good / better / best quotes, changeout proposals, tune-up planner, maintenance checklists, a leave-behind report, warranty registrations, permits, callbacks and history, phone tickets, technician hours to Back Office time cards, refrigerant recovery and cylinder records with a repair clock and year-end list, and the backflow test report on your water district's own form (bring the district's form and use Map the Form once to set it up). The refrigerant rules built in have not yet been reviewed against the regulations: check its thresholds and dates before you rely on them.
- Money rounds the normal way everywhere (a 5 rounds up): bids, proposals, assemblies, labor rates, pay applications, retainage, Project Manager and the bid pages. Each line is rounded the same way as its total, so lines add up to the total to the cent. A proposal shows the same price in every format. Number fields refuse what they can't read ("1.000,50", more than a trillion) instead of guessing, and Service refuses an amount over $100 million.
- Estimates: a unit cost typed on a takeoff line now carries into the proposal, the bid build-up, the exports and Build From Estimate, under its cost type (material when none is picked). Before, it counted in the Estimate total only. A measured quantity is priced as shown, to 2 decimals, so quantity x unit cost checks by hand; a quantity you type is priced exactly as typed. Both can move the total on an existing job by a few cents, or by the typed prices that were left out: re-check open bids. If a group is priced by an assembly and also has a unit cost typed on its line, both count: use one.
- Display: Rotate View fits the turned page; column headers and window titles that were cut off are shown in full; a unit cost shows as typed (4.125, not 4.13); Work Order fields are labelled.
- Pay applications: each line's retainage adds up to the retainage on the summary page. A little work never prints as "0%".
- Assemblies: formulas from an imported price list are checked once and repaired where an old version read them wrong.
- AI Summary, Redline and Spec Check read the whole document by default and say what they read. Redline and Spec Check show how long each part has waited, skip a part that doesn't answer in 5 minutes, can be stopped (Stop, then Stop Now), and say exactly which pages were not reviewed. Summary has a Stop button and the same 5-minute limit. The summary lists the sheets in the set apart from sheets only referenced, takes out abbreviation meanings the drawings don't define, and marks a sheet number or drawing scale that isn't in the drawings. It is still a draft to check against the drawings.
- Translate Markups runs in the background, keeps its result, says how many comments came back translated, and Cancel really ends the request.
- AI server set by your IT department: the Local AI Models window follows it too.
- Protected PDFs stay protected: Reduce File Size, Watermark and batch actions keep the password and permissions, and never change the file you have open. Reduce File Size uses less memory on big files and keeps the original when the result isn't smaller.
- Copy Back to the job folder no longer fails with "file in use" after the drawing was open in a tab.
- ADA checker: faster on big sets, its counts can be undone and are shared in a live session, and a parking stall marked both Parking and Accessible counts once.
- Accessibility Check: each automatic fix is its own Undo step; fixing no longer resets the viewer settings saved in the file.
- Aerial and site layers: a clear message when a map service sends too much or something unreadable; a rotated or stretched image file is refused instead of placed at the wrong scale.
- Project Manager: a save that happened while you were still typing could fail; fixed.
- Export to Excel keeps leading zeros in codes. Card-number search for redaction no longer flags ordinary long numbers.
- Back Office: the billing register refreshes after a change.
- Help: four pages pointed at the wrong tab; fixed.
