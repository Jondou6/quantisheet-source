QuantiSheet 1.1.15
- AI Takeoff reads the work notes too: the counts the notes state (INSTALL: 2 - MANUAL TRANSFER SWITCH, keyed notes, one-line feeder tags) come out as quantities in the same run. A plain count is taken off; a length, a note with something to check, or a count another step of the takeoff already made waits in review, so nothing is counted twice without a look.
- AI Takeoff on scanned floor plans: a scanned plan with a scale now has its rooms traced from the picture instead of being left to measure by hand. A scan with no readable room names says to run Make Searchable (OCR) first. Scans of details and sections are still measured by hand.
- The Estimate panel follows AI Takeoff: quantities it works out rather than draws (steel weight, wall area, finishes, door frames, work-note counts) show as takeoff lines with their CSI code. An estimate line linked to one follows when you run AI Takeoff again, and items you accept in review join straight away.
- Estimate > Export Bid Package: the proposal, the estimate summary (PDF and Excel), the estimate detail, the takeoff sheets with their measurements and, after AI Takeoff, the estimate workbook - in one folder and one zip to send. A contents list says what is in it and what still needs a look: an unlocked bid, lines with no price, AI items not checked.
- Source code (GNU AGPL-3.0): https://github.com/Jondou6/quantisheet-source/tree/v1.1.15 - the full source of this build. Help > About shows the same link.
