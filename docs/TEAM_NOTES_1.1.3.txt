QuantiSheet 1.1.3
- Auto Takeoff rooms: much more accurate. Each room's outline is now put back on the drawing's own wall lines, so small rooms and closets no longer measure an inch short all round. On our hard test plans every tagged room is found and 99% are within 2% of the true area (before: two rooms in three found, one in five within 2%). Rooms behind shut, double, pocket and bifold doors, closets, shafts and rooms with angled walls are found. A table or a toilet is no longer read as a door swing. Areas are still a draft to check: a room the program is unsure of is flagged.
- Auto Takeoff walls: walls are paired pen by pen, so a wall face is no longer paired with a counter edge or a fixture. Wall length on our test plans went from 7.6% over to within 2%, in the right thicknesses.
- Faster on big jobs: a markup on a sheet with 500 markups (about twice as fast), entering progress on a pay application late in a job (about six times as fast), switching theme and Field Mode. Files with large estimates and billing save smaller.
- Sturdier: damaged or odd markup data in a file is skipped instead of stopping the page; an amount too large to be money is refused with a message; a number field holding something that is not a number counts as zero in the bid build-up.
