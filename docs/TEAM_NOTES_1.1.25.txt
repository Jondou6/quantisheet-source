QuantiSheet 1.1.25
- Project Analysis (a button on the Home tab): open the specifications and click it. It answers the bid questions - project name, address, budget (not listed when the specs don't say), substantial and final completion, liquidated damages, project summary, bid date, pre-bid meeting (mandatory or optional) and retainage - then bid, performance and payment bonds, general liability insurance, the last day for questions, how bids are delivered, prevailing wage, DBE / MBE / SBE goals, alternates, unit prices, the number of addenda, the Owner and the Architect. Each answer shows its spec section and page (double-click to go there); nothing is guessed - an answer the specs don't give says not found. Where an addendum changes something, the newest addendum wins and the earlier answer is shown as also stated. Type the notice-to-proceed date to see the completion dates, and the days late to see the liquidated damages exposure. The answers are kept in the PDF (Run Again after adding an addendum); Copy, or Export PDF for a one-page summary.
- Calculators: four more tabs with Add to Estimate - Earthwork (a cut or a pipe trench: bank, loose and compacted CY, truck loads, bedding and backfill), Aggregate / Asphalt (tons of stone, sand, topsoil or hot mix over an area), Drywall (boards, tape, joint compound and screws) and Paint (gallons of finish and primer, in five-gallon pails). Every rule of thumb is a field you can change.
