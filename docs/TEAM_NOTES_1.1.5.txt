QuantiSheet 1.1.5
- Variance against our own estimate, line by line: a Variance button on the Leveling Board and on Scope Sheets. For each scope row it shows our number, each bidder's number and the difference in $ and %, and marks rows HIGH or LOW at a percent you set (15% to start; it is kept as your setting). Scope we carry that a bidder didn't price or excluded is listed per bidder at our dollar value, the same dollars the leveling plugs. A blank is not $0.00, and a row we have no number for says no estimate instead of 0%. The rows add up to the leveled total to the cent. Export to Excel or PDF.
- Bid forms: a new line type, Scope item, that each bidder marks Included or Excluded on the bid page (template: Scope checklist). Bidders now list inclusions, exclusions and clarifications one per line. On a Scope Sheet, the from-package button also brings in each bidder's answers: included / excluded flags, prices, and their exclusions and clarifications as Pending rows - nothing retyped, and cells you already filled in are left alone. Lines you ask on every package (bid bond, addenda, schedule) can be kept once per project: Bid Form > Project-wide lines. Print Bid Form saves the form as a PDF in the project's Bid Forms folder; nothing is sent. If you host your own hub server, update it so bidders see the Included / Excluded choice; the program tells you when an invitation went through an older hub.
- Bid Board (Estimate > Bid Board): every invitation to bid that comes to you in one list - project, GC, trade, bid date and time, job walk, questions due, estimator, your notes, and status (Undecided, Accepted, Submitted, Won, Lost, Declined). Add one by typing it in, or From Email to read a saved invitation (.eml, or pasted text) into a draft you check and save; nothing is sent and the email is not changed. An invitation that looks like a project already on the board (a similar name with a close bid date, or the same address, even from another GC) is flagged with the reason, and rows for the same project are marked so it is priced once. Go / No-Go gives a score and a recommendation from a short checklist with weights you can change; you decide, and your reason is kept. The Calendar tab lists the dates and exports a calendar file (.ics). Results shows win rate by GC (Won and Lost only), bids per month and each estimator's open bids; the Estimating Dashboard's hit rate can be grouped by GC (bid to). An Outlook .msg file isn't read: save the email as .eml or paste its text.
- Fill From the Job and Sign (Fill & Sign menu), for forms that come back to be filled and signed: each fillable field is listed with the value it would get and where that value comes from (your company settings, the job's billing info, the Company Directory for a sub's form, today's date). Fields the program is sure of start ticked; ones it is unsure of are marked to check and start unticked; a field it can't place is left for you. Tax IDs, Social Security numbers and bank details are never kept or filled. Fill Ticked writes a new file and leaves your form as it was. Sign Selected Box puts your saved signature in that one box, in a new file, and records who signed, when and the file's fingerprint in the file's audit trail and in Signed Forms Log.csv beside it. It is a drawn signature, not a certificate signature. Nothing is sent.
- Your drawn signature and initials are no longer kept in the settings file as plain text: they are stored encrypted for your Windows account, and moved there the first time they are used.
